### 🚀 Research Vision: Towards the World Model
Since I first encountered Diffusion Models in 2023, I have been captivated by their immense generative power. I believe that **generative capability is the ultimate goal for achieving AGI**. In my philosophy, achieving "controllable generation at will" is the definitive path to constructing a true **World Model**. To perfectly control the generation of a world, the model must inherently possess a profound and comprehensive understanding of that world's physics, semantics, and dynamics.

My representative projects include [**OmniDirector**](https://ymlinfeng.github.io/OmniDirector.github.io/) (multi-shot camera cloning on Kling Omni), [**TARS**](https://ymlinfeng.github.io/TARS.github.io/) (3D-free video re-shooting), [**ARGUS**](https://ymlinfeng.github.io/ARGUS.github.io/) (subject-ID control, NeurIPS 2026), [**Kling 3.0**](https://klingai.com/) (Subject-ID & Motion Control), [**Orpaint**](https://www.sciengine.com/doi/10.1007/s11432-024-4113-x) (Visual Mamba-based inpainting), and [**Make-a-Game**](https://github.com/YMlinfeng/MAG) (interactive game video generation).

### 🔍 Research Interests
I am focusing on **video generation** and **video foundation models**:
- 1️⃣ **Reference-to-Video (R2V) & Controllable Generation**: identity, motion, camera, dynamics, multi-subject, and voice/timbre references; video editing; Omni-style unified architectures.
- 2️⃣ **Video Foundation Models**: cross-pair data construction, data augmentation / filtering / generation, and DiT-based model design.
- 3️⃣ **Long Video, Reaction, and Physical Consistency**: making generated worlds stay coherent over time and under control.
