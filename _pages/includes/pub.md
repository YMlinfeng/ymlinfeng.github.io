[**SCA 2026**] [ParaScale: Scale-Calibrated Camera-Motion Transfer via a Gauge-Invariant Parallax Number](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19805) \\
**Zijie Meng**. \\
[[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19805) [[ResearchPod]](https://researchpod.app/paper/2f993ac5-f452-4e6f-996a-45db71c729de) <span style="color:red"><strong>(CCF-B, Computer Animation)</strong></span>

- We introduce the **Parallax Number ($\Pi$)**, a dimensionless, gauge-invariant descriptor that enables scale-faithful camera-motion transfer across disparate scenes (e.g., from galaxy sweeps to desk nudges).
- **ParaScale** is a plug-and-play, training-free module that re-realizes camera moves against target depths, cutting Parallax Consistency Error (PCE) by over **3×**.

</div>
</div>
